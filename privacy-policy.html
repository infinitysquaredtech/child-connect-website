Privacy Policy

Protecting your privacy is our highest priority | Last updated:

This Privacy Policy describes how Infinity Squared Technologies collects, uses, maintains, and discloses information collected from users of the Child Connect Platform ("the Platform").

Information We Collect

We collect various types of information to provide and improve our services:

Account and Profile Data

  • Public Users: No personal data is collected (anonymous searches only)
  • Parents: Name (or pseudonym), email, password, device information, screening results
  • NGO Staff: Name, email, organization, branch, profile ID, verification documents (ID cards, etc.)
  • NGO Admins: All staff data plus administrative privileges
  • Super/Master Admins: Government identification, authorization credentials
  • Email Verification: One-time codes sent to your email address to confirm you control it. Codes are stored as salted hashes with a short expiry, never in plain text

Child Profile Information

Critically Important: Detailed child records are strictly isolated by NGO. Staff from different NGOs cannot view each other's data.

  • Medical history and disability details
  • Family information (anonymized for public searches)
  • Educational records
  • Photos for face recognition (processed, not stored indefinitely)
  • Location data (only NGO contact shown publicly, never specific addresses)

Usage and Device Data

  • IP addresses and device fingerprints (for security only)
  • Browsing patterns and search queries
  • Screen time and feature usage statistics
  • Cookies and local storage identifiers

Government Scheme Data

  • State preferences (for scheme notifications)
  • Disability category interests
  • Subscription preferences for scheme updates
  • Notification history

How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for the following purposes:

Primary Operational Purposes

  • Provide and maintain platform functionality
  • Manage child profiles with NGO data isolation
  • Process face recognition matching for missing children
  • Deliver government scheme notifications
  • Conduct disability screenings and assessments
  • Verify your email address with a one-time code, and require a fresh code before sensitive actions such as changing contact details, deleting a child record, or approving an application

Security and Maintenance

  • Detect, prevent, and resolve security incidents
  • Verify the authenticity of user communications
  • Protect Platform integrity and availability
  • Analyze usage trends to improve services
  • Comply with legal obligations and regulatory requirements

Legal and Regulatory

Sharing data with law enforcement or government bodies when required by law to comply with legal obligations, protect rights, or prevent harm.

Data Security and Protection

Infinity Squared Technologies implements appropriate technical and organizational security measures:

Encryption and Access Controls:
  • Encryption in Transit: All data is encrypted using TLS/HTTPS protocols
  • Secure Storage: Sensitive data encrypted at rest using industry-standard algorithms
  • Role-Based Access: Strict permission levels ensure only authorized personnel can access specific data
  • NGO Isolation: Automatic data segregation between organizations
  • Session Management: Automatic logout after 6 hours of inactivity

Physical Security: Our servers and infrastructure are housed in secure facilities with restricted access and surveillance.

Employee Training: All staff with access to user data receive comprehensive privacy and security training.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law:

Standard Retention Periods

  • Account Data: Until account deletion or 7 years of account inactivity
  • Child Profiles: Indefinitely (required for ongoing care and reunification)
  • Usage Logs: 12 months for security analysis
  • Screening Data: Until user request or 5 years for research purposes

Deletion Process

  • Upon account deletion: Data securely erased from active databases
  • Backup data retained for 90 days then permanently deleted
  • Legal compliance data retained longer as required
  • Anonymous research data may be retained in aggregate form

Email verification codes

One-time verification codes are short-lived by design: each code expires within minutes (or as soon as it is used) and is deleted from our systems afterwards. Only a salted hash of each code is stored, so the record itself cannot be used to verify an address.

User Rights and Choices

You have rights regarding your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at contact@child-connect.in.

Critical Note: Due to the sensitive nature of child welfare data, some features may require NGO administration assistance. If you are a staff member or admin, coordinate with your organization for data requests.

Right to Access

  • Request a copy of your personal data held on our Platform
  • Review data usage patterns and history
  • Verify accuracy of stored information

Right to Rectification

  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of personal information (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Object to processing of data

Right to Data Portability

  • Request data transfer to another service in machine-readable format
  • Request data export for offline storage (limited to non-isolated NGO data)

Right to Erasure

  • "Right to be Forgotten" - request deletion of data (subject to data isolation requirements)
  • Children's data: Additional protections apply - parents/guardians can request deletion
  • NGO staff: Request data removal from specific organization

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent personal data to third parties. We may share data in the following circumstances:

Service Providers and Partners

  • Cloud hosting and data storage providers (e.g., Amazon Web Services, Azure)
  • Payment processors and billing services
  • Analytics and monitoring services (aggregated, anonymous data only)
  • Government and law enforcement with proper legal authority

Government Scheme Partners: We share anonymized data with government agencies to improve scheme delivery, but never individual identifiers without explicit consent or legal requirement.

NGO Data Isolation Guarantee: We absolutely never share NGO-specific data between organizations. Each NGO maintains complete sovereignty over their child profiles and data.

Children's Privacy: COPPA & DPDP Compliance

Child Connect is a child welfare platform. We take children's privacy extremely seriously and comply with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and the DPDP Act (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, India), along with other applicable laws protecting minors.

COPPA Compliance

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent
  • Screening assessments use pseudonyms/pseudonyms rather than real names
  • NGO staff must verify parental/guardian identity for child profile creation
  • We have a dedicated children's privacy program

DPDP Act Compliance

  • We comply with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) for all personal data processed through our platform
  • We obtain verifiable consent from parents or lawful guardians before processing personal data of children under 18, except where a legitimate use applies under the DPDP Act
  • We provide clear notice before collection and process data only for lawful, disclosed purposes
  • We do not undertake tracking, behavioral monitoring, or targeted advertising directed at children
  • We maintain reasonable security safeguards and a grievance redressal mechanism for data principals

Parents' and Guardians' Rights: Parents and lawful guardians can exercise data principal rights under the DPDP Act — including access, correction, and erasure of their children's data — at any time by contacting our data protection officer or administration.

Security Incidents and Breaches

We follow industry best practices for security and will respond promptly to any data breach. In the event of a data breach affecting personal information:

  • We will conduct a thorough investigation to determine the scope and impact
  • We will notify affected individuals and regulatory authorities as required by law
  • We will offer credit monitoring services where appropriate
  • We will work with law enforcement and cybersecurity experts to remediate the incident

If you suspect a security breach or unauthorized access, immediately contact contact@child-connect.in.

International Data Transfers

Data transferred across international borders is protected by the following safeguards:

Adequacy and Safeguards:
  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) with cloud providers
  • Government approval for cross-border data flows
  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest
  • Data localization where legally required
  • Compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP)

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in practices, legal requirements, or business operations. We will notify you of significant changes by:

  • Posting the new Privacy Policy on this page with an updated "Last updated" date
  • Sending notifications via email (where we have your contact information)
  • Displaying a prominent notice on the Platform

Your Continued Use: Your continued use of the Platform after changes constitutes acceptance of the modified Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with the changes, please discontinue use.
