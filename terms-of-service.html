Scope of Services

The Platform provides the following services:

1. Child Profile Management

  • For NGOs: Secure digital management of child records with automatic data isolation between different NGOs
  • For Parents: Access to profile management features (subject to appropriate permissions)
  • For Public Users: Anonymous search functionality to locate missing children using face recognition technology

2. Government Schemes Portal

  • Scheme Notifications: Access to government schemes relevant to children with disabilities
  • State & Category Filtering: Search and filter schemes by state and disability type
  • Subscription Feature: Opt-in notifications for new schemes in your state/interest area

3. Disability Screening Services

  • Autism Screening: M-CHAT-R/F assessments for toddlers
  • ADHD Screening: Vanderbilt Scale assessments for school-age children
  • Intellectual Disability Screening: Milestone assessments for all ages
  • Risk Assessment: Instant feedback and recommendations based on assessment results

4. Public Search with Face Recognition

  • Photo Match: Upload photos of missing children for facial recognition matching
  • NGO Contact Only: Match results reveal NGO contact information only, not specific locations
  • Privacy Protection: Detailed profiles remain isolated and inaccessible to unauthorized parties

5. Nearby NGO Locator

  • Location-Based Search: Uses your current location to find NGOs near you
  • No Location Storage: Your location is never stored or saved on our servers
  • Single Purpose Use: Location data is used solely for finding nearby NGOs and for no other purpose
