How It Works

How Child Connect Works

A streamlined flow from registration to impact. Bringing all stakeholders together on one platform.

01

Register & Setup

Parents, NGOs, and staff register on the platform. Parents submit applications with ID proof, while NGOs set up branches and onboard staff. Super admins configure the ecosystem. Every contact email is verified with a one-time code before an application is accepted.

02

Add Child Records

Staff create secure digital profiles with medical history, photos, disability information, and family details.

03

Screen & Discover

Parents run disability screenings, browse NGO directories, and get matched with relevant government welfare schemes. Applications are reviewed by super admins for approval.

04

Connect & Protect

Face recognition helps reunite missing children. Analytics drive informed decisions. Every child gets support.

For Parents

Medically-Standardized Screening Tools

Empowering parents with clinically validated assessments they can complete from home. Supporting early identification and timely intervention.

Autism Spectrum

M-CHAT-R/F

Modified Checklist for Autism in Toddlers, Revised with Follow-Up — the gold standard for early autism screening in children aged 16–30 months.

  • 20-question validated assessment
  • Risk stratification (Low / Medium / High)
  • Follow-up interview protocol
ADHD

Vanderbilt Scale

Vanderbilt ADHD Diagnostic Rating Scale — a comprehensive tool assessing inattention, hyperactivity, and conduct symptoms.

  • Parent-reported behavioral scoring
  • Inattentive & hyperactive subtypes
  • Performance impairment metrics
Intellectual Disability

Milestone Assessment

Developmental milestone tracking for age-appropriate skill evaluation across motor, language, social, and cognitive domains.

  • Age-specific milestone checklists
  • Multi-domain evaluation
  • Progress tracking over time

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for NGOs, parents, and institutions evaluating Child Connect.

How can parents register on Child Connect?

Parents can apply by clicking Register as Parent in the footer or visiting the Parent Application Page. Applications require ID proof (Aadhaar, Passport, etc.) and are reviewed by super admins before account creation.

Who can use Child Connect?

Child Connect supports NGO administrators, NGO staff, parents or guardians, and government teams working on disability support programs.

Does Child Connect include parent screening tools?

Yes. The platform includes M-CHAT-R/F, Vanderbilt ADHD screening, and developmental milestone assessments for early support pathways.

How is child data protected?

Role-based access, secure sessions, and NGO-level data isolation ensure sensitive records remain private and visible only to authorized users.

How are email addresses verified?

When you submit an application, reset a password, or confirm a sensitive action (such as changing your contact details, deleting a record, or approving an application), Child Connect emails a short one-time code to your address. The action completes only after the code is entered, so requests stay tied to addresses their owners actually control. Codes expire within minutes and are never stored in plain text. Questions? Email contact@child-connect.in.

How do we request onboarding or a demo?

Use the Request a Demo action below or email contact@child-connect.in to start your onboarding discussion.

Can multiple NGO branches use Child Connect separately?

Yes. Child Connect supports multi-branch NGO structures with organization-level data isolation for secure and separate operations.

Does Child Connect help parents find government schemes?

Yes. Parents can discover welfare schemes by state and disability type, and stay updated through the platform's parent-focused workflows.
